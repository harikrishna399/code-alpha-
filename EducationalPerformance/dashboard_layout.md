# Educational Dashboard Layout

## Page 1 — Overview
KPI cards: Student Count, Attendance %, Overall Score, Credit Completion %.
Bar charts: average score and attendance by department.
Slicers: academic year, term, department, year of study.

## Page 2 — Subject Results
Clustered columns: Math, Programming, Communication scores by department.
Matrix: department × term.
Scatter: attendance vs overall score.

## Page 3 — Resource Allocation
Bar charts: library visits and lab hours by department.
Support flag counts at aggregate level.
Use clear caveats that the sample is synthetic and associations are not causal.
