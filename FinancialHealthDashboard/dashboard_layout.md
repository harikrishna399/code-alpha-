# Financial Dashboard — Visual Layout

Use a 16:9 canvas and a clean navy/teal theme.

## Page 1: Executive Overview
Top row: four KPI cards — Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Net Profit, Profit Margin %.
Middle left: monthly Revenue and Net Profit line chart.
Middle right: Actual Revenue vs Budget clustered column chart.
Bottom: Net Profit by Department bar chart and a month/department detail table.
Slicers across the top: Date range and Department.

## Page 2: Budget & Expenses
- Line/column chart: Actual Revenue and Revenue Budget by month.
- Column chart: Actual Expenses and Expense Budget by month.
- Stacked column chart: Operating Expenses vs Other Expenses.
- Matrix: Month, Department, Revenue, Revenue Budget, Total Expenses, Expense Budget, Net Profit.

## Page 3: Cash Position
- Line chart: Opening Cash and Closing Cash by month.
- Table with monthly opening and closing balances.
- Add a note that sample cash balances are illustrative; cash flow requires transaction-level cash receipts and payments.

## Accessibility and formatting
- Use descriptive visual titles and currency formatting.
- Avoid relying on color alone to communicate positive/negative variance.
- Add alt text to key visuals where available.
- Keep slicer behavior consistent across pages and test interactions.
