# HR Analytics — Visual Layout

Use a clean, accessible design with restrained blue/teal accents. Keep personal employee-level details off public pages.

## Page 1: Workforce Overview
Top row: Total Employees, Active Employees, Exited Employees, Average Satisfaction.
Middle: Active Employees by Department bar chart; Employment Status donut chart.
Bottom: Average Performance Rating by Department and Average Training Hours by Department.
Slicers: Department, Location, Job Role.

## Page 2: Recruitment & Exits
- Hires by year/month using HireDate.
- Recruitment Source distribution.
- Exited employee counts by department and exit year using ExitDate.
- Include a visible definition note for every attrition/turnover KPI.

## Page 3: Performance & Engagement
- Average Satisfaction by Department.
- Average Performance Rating by Department.
- Scatter chart: Training Hours vs Performance Rating.
- Optional aggregated salary by role for restricted audiences only.

## Important analytical caveats
The supplied sample contains 20 synthetic employee records and is for demonstrating report construction only. It is too small for robust prediction. Add monthly headcount snapshots, requisition openings, applications, hires, and time-to-fill history before building a defensible hiring forecast.

## Accessibility and privacy
Use readable font sizes, clear titles, alt text, and keyboard-friendly slicers where supported. Avoid publishing employee identifiers or individual salary details.
