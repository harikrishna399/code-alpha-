# Real Estate Market Trends

## Objective
Compare property prices, rental yields, supply and demand, economic-rate context and geographic hotspots.

## Data files
- property_market_data.csv: 72 synthetic neighborhood-month observations across three cities in 2025.
- DAX_Measures.dax: reference price, rent, gross yield, demand/supply and month-over-month measures.
- dashboard_layout.md: report layout.
- dashboard.html and geographic_hotspots.html: supplementary browser previews.

## PBIP source
Open ../PowerBIProjects/RealEstateMarket/RealEstateMarket.pbip in Power BI Desktop. Latitude and Longitude are provided for map visual practice.

## Report pages
1. Market Overview: average sale price, rent, gross yield, supply/demand, trend and city comparisons.
2. Rental Yield & Geographic Hotspots: neighborhood yield, geographic visual, and location detail.

## Definitions and limits
Gross rental yield = monthly rent × 12 / sale price; excludes costs and vacancy. Demand/supply ratio = transactions / listings. Coordinates represent approximate neighborhood centers, not actual listings. All market values are synthetic and not real-time data. Validate the geographic visual in Power BI Desktop and save the native PBIX when successful.
