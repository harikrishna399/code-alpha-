# Real Estate Dashboard Layout

## Page 1 — Market Overview
KPI cards: Average Sale Price, Average Monthly Rent, Gross Rental Yield.
Line chart: sale price by month.
Clustered columns: listings vs transactions by city.
Slicers: month, city, neighborhood, property type.

## Page 2 — Rental Yield
Bar chart: gross yield by neighborhood.
Scatter plot: price vs rent.
Table: price, monthly rent, annualized rent, yield.

## Page 3 — Supply and Demand
Line chart: listings and transactions over time.
Matrix: city/neighborhood with demand-to-supply ratio.
Map: only after verifying geocoding.

Use INR formatting, descriptive titles, accessible contrast, and visible caveats about synthetic data.
